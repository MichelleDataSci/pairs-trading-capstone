MEAN REVERSION ASSESSMENT:

IMPORTANT NOTE:
  The Z-score in Phase 4 uses a fixed mu and sigma anchored to 2025.
  This is stricter than Phase 3b (which used a rolling 30-day window).
  A fixed anchor is more conservative -- any drift in the spread level
  or variance in 2026 will show up as Z-score deviation, even if the
  pair remains broadly cointegrated.
