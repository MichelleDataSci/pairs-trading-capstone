
PAIRS TRADING STRATEGY -- CONCLUSION
=====================================

COINTEGRATION:
  AMZN/META is the only pair among the 15 tested to pass both EG
  (p = 0.0144) and Johansen (trace = 17.52 vs 15.49 critical) at 5%.
  The cointegrating relationship only stabilised after 2020; in 2018-2019
  the hedge ratio R-squared was near zero.

STRATEGY PERFORMANCE (baseline: entry=+-2.0, window=30d, stop=+-3.0):
  Train 2018-2021  : Sharpe=0.21, Total PnL=+0.1816
  Test  2022-2025  : Sharpe=-0.12, Total PnL=-0.1544, MaxDD=-0.6531
  Walk-forward avg : Sharpe=0.18, 4 of 7 years profitable

BENCHMARK COMPARISON:
  Over the test period the pairs strategy underperformed directional
  buy-and-hold of AMZN, META, and the S&P 500 on a cumulative log-return
  basis. The strategy's value proposition is market-neutrality -- it does
  not carry directional market exposure -- at the cost of lower absolute
  returns.

PARAMETER SENSITIVITY:
  The best Sharpe ratio across all 36 parameter combinations tested is
  0.2572. No combination exceeds a Sharpe of 1.0 in the
  test period, confirming the result is not an artefact of parameter
  selection -- the strategy is only weakly profitable regardless of
  threshold, window, or stop-loss choice.

KEY LIMITATIONS:
  1. Cointegration alone is not sufficient for profitability. The
     statistical relationship is real but regime-dependent, and the
     hedge ratio requires annual recalibration.
  2. Transaction costs and regime instability reduce performance.
     At 10 bps/leg (already conservative), the net edge is thin.
  3. The max drawdown relative to P&L magnitude is large. Robust
     position sizing and risk management are essential for live trading.
  4. Phase 4 tests whether the AMZN/META cointegrating relationship holds
     on genuinely unseen 2026 data using a fixed Z-score anchor from 2025.
