
PAIRS TRADING STRATEGY -- CONCLUSION
=====================================

COINTEGRATION:
  AMZN/META is the only pair among the 15 tested to pass both EG
  (p = 0.0144) and Johansen (trace = 17.52 vs 15.49 critical) at 5%.
  However, this should be treated cautiously: with 15 pairs tested at a
  flat 5% threshold, ~0.75 false positives are expected by chance. AMZN/META
  does not survive Bonferroni correction (α = 0.05/15 = 0.0033), meaning the
  cointegration finding itself may be a statistical false positive.

STRATEGY PERFORMANCE (baseline: entry=±2.0, window=30d, stop=±3.0, cost=10bps):
  Train 2018-2021  : Sharpe=0.21, Total PnL=+0.1816
  Test  2022-2025  : Sharpe=-0.12, Total PnL=-0.1544, MaxDD=-0.6531
  Walk-forward avg : Sharpe=0.18, 4 of 7 years profitable

  The positive training Sharpe (0.21) likely reflects in-sample fitting
  rather than genuine edge. Without bootstrap confidence intervals it cannot
  be distinguished from zero at conventional significance levels.

  The walk-forward average of 0.18 deserves the same caveat. Each annual
  window generates only 4-8 trades (see strategy_results.csv), which is
  far too few to produce a reliable Sharpe estimate -- the standard error
  of a Sharpe ratio is approximately sqrt((1 + 0.5*SR^2) / n), so at n=6
  trades the uncertainty dwarfs the estimate itself. The figure should not
  be read as evidence of consistent edge. Additionally, the two highest-
  contributing years (2020: Sharpe=0.62, 2021: Sharpe=0.68) coincide with
  the COVID-era volatility spike, suggesting regime dependency rather than
  a repeatable structural relationship. The three consecutive negative years
  that follow (2022-2024) are consistent with the pair's cointegrating
  relationship breaking down in the post-COVID period.

COST SENSITIVITY (test period, baseline parameters):
  Cost (bps)   Sharpe    Total PnL
       5bps    -0.0513    -0.0664
      10bps    -0.1191    -0.1544
      20bps    -0.2538    -0.3304
      30bps    -0.3869    -0.5064

  The strategy produces negative Sharpe at every cost level tested,
  including 5 bps -- half the base assumption. This means the underperformance
  is not driven by transaction costs: the spread itself does not mean-revert
  reliably enough to generate profit in the test period before costs are even
  applied. The prior framing of "10 bps is already conservative" is
  misleading -- cost level is irrelevant when there is no gross edge.

PARAMETER SENSITIVITY (36 combinations, test period):
  35 of 36 combinations produce negative Sharpe in the test period.
  The 3 positive-Sharpe combinations (Sharpe: 0.20, 0.24, 0.26) all use
  entry=2.5, window=90d, and generate only 6 trades over 4 years. Six trades
  is not a sufficient sample to draw any inference about strategy profitability;
  these results are statistical noise, not evidence of edge.

  Honest summary: no parameter combination tested produces a reliably
  positive Sharpe in the out-of-sample period with an adequate trade count.

BENCHMARK COMPARISON:
  Over the test period the pairs strategy underperformed directional
  buy-and-hold of AMZN, META, and the S&P 500. The strategy's theoretical
  value proposition is market-neutrality; in practice it delivered neither
  market-neutral returns nor capital preservation.

KEY LIMITATIONS:
  1. Cointegration finding is statistically fragile. It does not survive
     Bonferroni correction for multiple comparisons across 15 pairs.
  2. No gross edge in the test period. The strategy loses money before
     transaction costs, so cost management cannot rescue it.
  3. Regime instability. Phase 4 (2026 out-of-sample) confirms the
     AMZN/META relationship did not hold on genuinely unseen data.
  4. Single pair, single sector. All conclusions rest on one relationship
     in one industry. Generalisation cannot be assessed from this study alone.
  5. All reported Sharpe ratios (train=0.21, walk-forward avg=0.18,
     individual years=0.04 to 0.68) lack bootstrap confidence intervals
     and trade counts too small to be reliable. None should be cited as
     evidence of edge without those intervals applied consistently.
