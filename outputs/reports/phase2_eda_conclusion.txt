
EDA CONCLUSION -- PHASE 2 SUMMARY
==================================

Based on eight years (2018-2025) of daily data for six S&P 500 tech stocks:

RETURN STATISTICS:
  - NVDA dominates in both return (0.233%/day) and volatility (48.3% ann.).
    Its AI/GPU-driven 37x price surge from mid-2023 makes it a poor candidate
    for any stable long-term pair relationship.
  - META shows extreme kurtosis (18.12), driven by its Feb-2022 single-day
    -26% collapse. Any META-involving pair carries asymmetric spread-gap risk.

BENCHMARK SENSITIVITY (BETA):
  - All six stocks are market-amplifying (beta > 1.0). MSFT (1.18) and
    GOOGL (1.16) have the most stable beta profiles, making their price-level
    relationship easier to model, though correlation alone does not imply
    cointegration.

VIX SENSITIVITY:
  - All stocks are negatively correlated with VIX returns (stocks fall when
    fear rises). On high-VIX days (VIX > 25) annualised volatility roughly
    doubles vs low-VIX regimes. Strategy signals during high-VIX periods
    carry elevated false-signal risk.

ROLLING CORRELATION:
  - 60-day rolling correlations are highly regime-dependent. Most pairs show
    correlation spikes during the 2020 COVID crash and 2022 bear market,
    and troughs in 2023-2024 as NVDA decoupled from peers.

PAIRS MOST LIKELY TO BE COINTEGRATED (EDA view):
  1. AMZN/META -- moderate return correlation (0.57), both driven by
     consumer/advertiser cycle. Neither has undergone the AI structural break
     seen in NVDA. Price scatter shows a reasonable linear trend.
  2. MSFT/AAPL -- highest price-level R^2 (0.966), similar betas, stable
     rolling correlation over time.
  3. MSFT/GOOGL -- highest return correlation (0.709), similar business
     models (search, cloud, enterprise SaaS).

CAUTION FLAGS:
  - NVDA pairs: expected to fail cointegration due to the AI-driven
    structural break from mid-2023.
  - 2022 bear market is the critical stress test for all backtests.
    META fell 65%, AMZN 50%, NVDA 55% in that calendar year.
